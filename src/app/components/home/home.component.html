100% Client-Side Privacy & Data Sovereignty Guarantee UFX Studio operates as a self-contained browser application. All image mockup processing, color extraction, typography styling, PDF guideline compilation, and TTF font ZIP generation take place strictly inside your local browser runtime environment. No user mockups, brand assets, or design tokens are ever transmitted to an external server or stored in a remote database.

1. Zero Telemetry & Tracking

We do not execute tracking scripts, user session recorders, or analytics trackers. Your design workflow remains private and unmonitored.

2. Local Storage Ownership

Your active design system configuration is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. You maintain 100% ownership over your brand files.

3. Direct Open Asset Exports

Exported JSON design tokens, PDF guideline documents, and `.zip` font packages are generated locally and downloaded directly to your device.

4. Open Source CDN Requests

Web font stylesheets and raw font binaries are fetched directly via standard CORS requests to official Google Fonts servers (fonts.googleapis.com).

5. No Account Authentication

UFX Studio requires no registration, password creation, email collection, or subscription signup. You can use the entire platform anonymously.

6. Commercial & Personal Rights

All brand guidelines, color palettes, and component tokens created with UFX Studio belong exclusively to you for unlimited commercial and personal use.

Q: What is UFX Studio? UFX Studio (Universal Framework & Xecution) is a free-hand design-to-code platform. It allows designers and developers to build brand guidelines, configure semantic UI tokens, analyze mockups, and export production assets.
Q: Are exported fonts free for commercial use? Yes! All font families in UFX Studio are open-source fonts hosted by Google Fonts under open licenses (SIL Open Font License or Apache 2.0). You are free to use them in commercial, personal, web, and print projects.
Q: Can I import my saved JSON foundations back later? Yes! On Step 01 (Choose Your Builder), select Import Configuration and upload your exported -foundations.json file to instantly restore your entire brand configuration.
Q: How does the AI Mockup Analysis work? You can upload up to 5 image mockups. The built-in client-side image analyzer scans pixel data, samples dominant colors, classifies primary/secondary/neutral/functional tones, and automatically generates 9-step color scales.
